Definition

What is Inclusion?

Inclusion means ensuring that every person — regardless of their gender, caste, religion, income, disability, or geographic location — has equal access to opportunities, resources, and dignity.

In the context of economic inclusion, it means making sure that marginalised communities can participate fully in the economy: finding decent work, accessing education, and benefiting from government support systems.

Root Causes

Why Does Poverty Persist?

Poverty is not a personal failure — it is a systemic issue rooted in unequal access and structural barriers.

Lack of Quality Education

Millions of children drop out due to financial hardship, distance, or lack of infrastructure. Without education, skill development stagnates and opportunities remain out of reach.

265M children out of school globally

Information Asymmetry

Most people in rural areas and urban slums have no knowledge of government schemes, scholarships, or skill programs they are entitled to — simply because information doesn't reach them.

70% of eligible Indians miss out on welfare benefits

Geographic & Digital Barriers

Remote populations face physical isolation from economic centers. The digital divide further excludes communities from online job markets, e-learning, and digital banking.

500M Indians lack internet access

Gender & Social Discrimination

Women, SC/ST communities, and minorities face systemic discrimination in hiring, wages, and education access — perpetuating cycles of poverty across generations.

India ranks 127th in gender equality index

Lack of Skill-Job Alignment

Millions of youth possess informal skills but cannot translate them into formal employment because they lack certifications, soft skills, or knowledge of market demand.

47% of Indian graduates are unemployable
